# 🎬 3-Minute Devpost Demo Video Script: ActorRoom Live

> **Format:** Screen capture with live microphone audio (No face cam required, or picture-in-picture).  
> **Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds (Well within the 3-minute hard limit).  
> **Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges (Google Gemini & Replit Agent Track).

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Walkthrough

### [0:00 - 0:30] Introduction & The Problem
* **Visual:** Open the dark-mode ActorRoom Live application (`http://localhost:8000`).
* **Spoken Pitch:**
  > *"Hi everyone! When film and television actors prepare self-tape auditions, almost every scene involves 2 to 4 characters. To record a great audition, actors need an off-camera reader to speak opposing dialogue with authentic timing and emotion.*  
  > *Today, actors either pay $50 an hour for human readers on WeRehearse, beg untrained roommates, or use robotic apps that just replay recorded files separated by awkward silences.*  
  > *This is **ActorRoom Live**—an ultra-low-latency multi-agent audition sandbox that gives actors real-time AI dialogue partners with natural turn-taking, distinct human voices, sub-150ms barge-in cutoffs, and post-take director coaching."*

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Demo 1: Scripted Table-Read & Barge-In Cutoff
* **Visual:** 
  - Select **`The Interrogation`** scene.
  - Set mode to **`📜 Scripted`**.
  - Set Your Role to **`DETECTIVE MILLER`**.
  - Click **`🎙️ Start Rehearsal`**.
* **Action:**
  - Read your opening line into your microphone:  
    *"Sit down, Viktor. We both know how this ends."*
  - Pause naturally.
  - Viktor responds in his authentic, cynical neural voice:  
    *"Do we, Detective? Because from where I'm sitting, your case fell apart an hour ago."*
  - While Viktor is delivering his next line, **interrupt him** loudly:  
    *"Don't play games with me!"*
  - **Point out:** Look at how Viktor's voice immediately cuts off in under 150 milliseconds. No robotic overlap!

---

### [1:15 - 1:55] Demo 2: 🎭 Live Gemini Conversational Improv
* **Visual:** 
  - Click the **`[ 🎭 Improv ]`** button toggle in the top bar.
  - Click **`🎙️ Start Rehearsal`**.
* **Action:**
  - Ask an unscripted question:  
    *"Viktor, you were seen at the docks at midnight. Who gave you the encrypted keycard?"*
  - Stop speaking.
  - Point to the teleprompter: *"Viktor is listening and formulating response..."*
  - Viktor replies in real time with a spontaneous, witty, in-character deflection generated by Gemini 2.5 Flash.
  - Show how the dialogue seamlessly responds to your unscripted questions.

---

### [1:55 - 2:25] Demo 3: 📄 PDF Audition Sides Drag-and-Drop
* **Visual:** 
  - Click the **`📄 Upload Sides`** button.
  - Select any screenplay PDF from your computer (e.g. `sample_scripts` or audition sides).
* **Action:**
  - Show how PyMuPDF instantly extracts the slugline, character names, and dialogue lines into the teleprompter.
  - Switch your character role to the newly parsed character with zero manual configuration.

---

### [2:25 - 2:45] Conclusion & 🏆 Post-Take Director Scorecard
* **Visual:** 
  - Click **`🎬 Cut & Review`**.
  - The **Director's Scorecard Modal** opens.
* **Spoken Pitch:**
  - Point to the **Pacing Score (e.g. 92%)**, **Line Accuracy (95%)**, and **Overall Callback Potential**.
  - Read the 3 specific notes generated by Gemini: Subtext intention, vocal pacing, and actionable advice for the next take.
  - Conclude:  
    > *"With sub-400ms latency, authentic neural voices, and instant barge-in cutoffs, ActorRoom Live is the future of actor audition preparation, built entirely with Gemini and ready to run on Replit with one click. Thank you!"*
